## Target population of DUQ200 (codebook 'Target' field)

| Cycle | Target |
|---|---|
| D | Both males and females 20 YEARS - 59 YEARS |
| E | Both males and females 20 YEARS - 59 YEARS |
| F | Both males and females 18 YEARS - 59 YEARS |
| G | Both males and females 18 YEARS - 59 YEARS |
| H | Both males and females 18 YEARS - 59 YEARS |
| I | Both males and females 18 YEARS - 59 YEARS |
| J | Both males and females 18 YEARS - 59 YEARS |

Note: although several DUQ documentation pages describe public data for ages 18–69,
in the public files every participant aged 60–69 is missing DUQ200 (verified in
`analysis/tests/testthat/test-harmonize.R`). Marijuana analyses are restricted to ≤59.
